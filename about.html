Where DiveTower comes from

DiveTower is built and used every single day at Koh Kood Divers, a PADI 5-Star dive centre on Koh Kood, a small island in Thailand, running since 2009. It didn't start as a product. It started as a fix — for lost bookings, missed pickups, salary arguments, and paper waivers that got wet on the boat.

Every feature exists because our own shop needed it. Every screen has been tested on real boats, real pickups, real customers, and real island internet. If something doesn't work in the rain on a pier with one bar of signal, we don't ship it.

Why only 50 shops

Because we answer our own support chat. We're a small team of dive shop owners and developers, and we want to know every shop on the platform by name — your setup, your boats, your season. That stops being possible at a certain size, so we've drawn the line at 50 dive shops worldwide. When the seats are taken, the door closes.

What that means for you

When you message us, you talk to people who have stood at the dive shop counter at 7 in the morning with three walk-ins, a missing instructor, and a boat leaving in an hour. We don't need your problem explained twice. And it goes further than support: we customize DiveTower to the needs of your shop. If your operation works differently, or you need something the app doesn't do yet, tell us — and we build it for you. That's what being one of 50 means.
