Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DiveTower, from setup to daily operations.

Setup & Basics

Q: What is DiveTower?
A: It's a complete management system that handles every step from when a customer books through payment, the dive, and paying staff after. Each role (owner, instructor, driver, kitchen, boat crew) gets their own app that all share the same live data.
Q: Will my data disappear if someone loses their phone?
A: No. All data is stored securely online, not on anyone's phone. Even if a phone breaks, your business data stays safe. The system automatically backs up everything every 30 minutes to a separate secure location.
Q: What if the internet goes down?
A: Staff apps still work offline. Actions save and send automatically once connection returns. Drivers and boat staff with bad signal can keep working without issues.
Q: I have an international customer base. What languages does the system support?
A: English, Thai, French, German, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Chinese, Korean, and Japanese. Customers see everything in their chosen language automatically - booking form, confirmation emails, their app, and waivers.
Q: Can I use different videos for different languages?
A: Yes. You can set a different preparation video for each language and the system shows each customer the right one automatically.
Q: Who can track who did what in the system?
A: The system records it all. Every payment shows who collected it. Every deleted booking shows who deleted it and why. Every salary payment shows who marked it paid. This is built in for accountability.
Q: My shop operates in multiple countries with different currencies. Can DiveTower handle that?
A: Yes. It supports over 100 world currencies and lets you set manual exchange rates for US dollars and euros.

Before Your First Day

Q: How do new staff members get set up quickly?
A: Each role gets its own QR code. A new staff member just scans it to install their app immediately - no email setup, no app store needed.
Q: What information do I need for each instructor?
A: Name, email, password, agency registration number, phone, WhatsApp, commission rate, nationality, languages they speak, a photo. You also set whether they can log in on days off, offer Pay Later, show their QR code, or show an open booking form. You can mark unavailable date ranges too.
Q: How do I set up drivers?
A: Same as instructors, but they need a maximum passenger number instead of languages.
Q: What about kitchen staff?
A: Much simpler - just name, phone, WhatsApp, email, and password.
Q: Do I need to set up each boat type?
A: Yes. Add boat names and their passenger capacity in Team & Staff settings.
Q: How do I tell the system my commission rates?
A: There's a general commission rate table, but you can override it for specific resorts or activities.
Q: Where do I add resort pickup times and commission rates?
A: The Resorts & Agents section. Add each resort's pickup time, email, commission rate. You also get automatic booking links and QR codes for each resort.
Q: How do I set up activities with pricing and structure?
A: Activities & Pricing section has everything in one place: price, number of days, salary, certification cost, agency, whether it needs a prerequisite. Below that, set the day-by-day structure - which dives happen on which day and what gear each day needs. Dive count calculates automatically from the structure.
Q: How do instructors use the activity structure?
A: They see it as a checklist they check off as the course happens, matching your activity's exact structure.
Q: How do I handle park fees that only apply some days?
A: Special Fee Day section. Set date ranges with patterns that can't overlap. Separate fees for diving vs snorkeling, split into local adult, local child, foreign adult, foreign child. You can turn fees on or off manually for any day too.
Q: What payment methods can I accept?
A: Cash, QR codes, Stripe, PayPal, PayMongo, Opn, HitPay. Each online provider just needs a connection link set up once. DiveTower can help set up others beyond these.
Q: What if I want to use custom waiver documents instead of the built-in ones?
A: You can upload your own waivers. They're automatically available in all ten languages.
Q: How does DiveTower handle my cancellation policy?
A: The Waivers section lets you set how many days notice is needed and when the last bookings of the day cutoff is. Staff and customers see exactly what text applies.

Before Each Day

Q: How do I set up which boats go out and when?
A: On the Day tab, create trips for each boat going out at a particular time. Set how many dive slots each trip has. Slots can be named freely or set to "Confined" with one tap. Yesterday's trips copy automatically.
Q: What tells me something is missing before I start the day?
A: Red fields on booking cards. They show: no instructor, no taxi driver, no boat pickup time, no resort, no meal, or no boat assigned.
Q: Can I fix multiple bookings at once?
A: Yes. Select many bookings at once and make bulk changes.
Q: How do I quickly check if everything is ready?
A: The Day tab shows every booking with their language, activity, instructor, pickup method and time, resort, meal, and boat assignment all at a glance.
Q: I want to assign a specific instructor to different dives within the same activity.
A: You can - each dive can have its own instructor if needed, different from the main instructor for the day.
Q: How do I track if a returning customer has all their info?
A: Their customer record keeps everything from before: contact details, gear sizes, diving level, history, meal preference, and waiver status. Gear sizes auto-calculate into BCD, wetsuit, and fin sizes. If medical info is missing, you get alerted.
Q: A customer's doctor cleared them to dive but has specific requirements.
A: They can upload a photo of their fit-to-dive form instead of just ticking medical approved. It can be multiple pages.
Q: How do I quickly add multiple people from an agent to one booking?
A: Fast form in Resorts & Agents: pick the agent, pick the date, and list the people. Shortcut to add several at once if they're doing the same activity.

Managing Money During the Day

Q: What did we make so far today?
A: Money Overview shows money paid today in a box at the top. Or look at the simple payment list for the day.
Q: How much do customers still owe us right now?
A: Money Overview displays this in the "money still owed by customers" box.
Q: Do we have money coming from agents or resorts that hasn't cleared?
A: Yes, Money Overview shows money owed to agents and resorts in its top summary.
Q: Are there refunds I decided to give that I haven't processed yet?
A: Money Overview highlights refunds still needing a decision in its top box.
Q: When a customer pays an agent directly, how does that affect what I owe the agent?
A: The system automatically removes that amount from your agent balance, so the math stays correct without manual adjusting.
Q: I need to collect money from a customer right now. How do I record a payment?
A: From the customer's booking card or detail screen, there's a way to record what they paid and how they paid it. Or show them a QR code for direct payment.

After the Day

Q: How much did each activity make this week?
A: Money Overview breaks down income by activity with a table and chart for any period you pick.
Q: Which payment method do most customers use?
A: Money Overview has a payment method breakdown for any time period.
Q: Did expensive activities make more money than budget ones?
A: Money Overview shows income by activity so you can compare directly.
Q: How much money is each instructor generating?
A: Income breakdown by instructor in Money Overview for any period.
Q: Are customers from certain countries spending more?
A: Money Overview breaks income down by nationality.
Q: How many meals are we serving per day on average?
A: Money Overview shows meal counts along with other summaries for any period.
Q: I need to bill an agent for a whole week of bookings at once.
A: Use Money Overview to send a combined invoice for all their bookings from that period - lists every student, date, and activity.
Q: Can I export this week's data to look at in a spreadsheet?
A: Yes. On computer, an Export button downloads current tab data as a spreadsheet. Works for Day tab, Details, Gear, Salary, Money Overview, Shop Settings, and DiveAgency.

Instructor & Staff Management

Q: How much does each instructor owe me right now?
A: The Salary tab shows each instructor's total owed with a breakdown per student. Custom dive icons show which dives are marked done for payment.
Q: An instructor completed their dives but I haven't marked them paid yet. Are they getting paid?
A: No. Incomplete work shows in a separate "pending" list, kept apart so it doesn't get mixed up with money ready to pay.
Q: If two instructors share one day, do they each get full pay?
A: No. Each instructor only gets paid for their own portion of the work.
Q: Can I pay all instructors at once?
A: Yes. One button marks everything as paid.

Chat & Communication

Q: Can staff in different roles message each other?
A: Yes. Group channels exist for each role: Masters, Instructors, Taxi, Kitchen, Boat Crew. Plus direct one-to-one messages between anyone.
Q: Can we send announcements to everyone?
A: Yes. An Announcements thread broadcasts to all staff.
Q: Do customers use a different chat?
A: Yes. General shop questions chat and a second chat that goes straight to the customer's current instructor if they have one.
Q: Can people send photos in chat?
A: Yes. Chat supports photos and shows read status.
Q: Do staff get notified about new messages?
A: Yes. Chat shows unread count in the app and on phones that support it, a badge on the home screen icon.

QR Codes & Booking Links

Q: Can I generate a QR code for each resort's own booking page?
A: Yes. Each resort gets its own automatic booking link and QR code in the Resorts & Agents section.
Q: What about an instructor's personal booking code for commission tracking?
A: Yes. Each instructor gets their own personal QR code and link. Bookings through it track back to them for commission.
Q: Can drivers and boat crew have their own booking codes?
A: Yes. Toggle on in their settings whether their app shows their personal QR code for bookings.
Q: Can the shop have a master QR code?
A: Yes. The QR tab has QR codes for the shop, each instructor, and each resort's booking page.
Q: What if someone doesn't have a QR scanner?
A: Every QR also has a link version they can click or type in.

Data Integrity & Safety

Q: What happens if we lose a phone?
A: No data is lost because everything is stored online, not on the phone.
Q: How often is data backed up?
A: Automatically every 30 minutes to a separate secure location, so there are always two safe copies.
Q: Can I restore a deleted booking?
A: Yes. Deleted bookings go to Trash and can be restored or permanently deleted.
Q: Who deleted a booking and why?
A: The system records who deleted it and shows the reason they gave.
Q: Can staff see that a message was read?
A: Yes. Chat shows if a message has been read.

Bulk Operations

Q: Can I print schedules or rosters?
A: Yes. On computer, a Print button prints whatever tab is open. Also a Roster button to print a clean roster for any single boat.
Q: Can I get data out as a spreadsheet?
A: Yes. Export downloads the current tab as spreadsheet. Works for: Day tab, Details, Gear, Salary, Money Overview, Shop Settings, DiveAgency.
Q: Which tabs can be exported?
A: Day, Details, Gear, Salary, Money Overview, Shop Settings, DiveAgency.

Late Bookings & Special Cases

Q: What's Pending Late?
A: Bookings made after your shop's daily cutoff time. They don't confirm automatically - a master must approve or reject each one.
Q: How do I handle bookings that come in too late?
A: Pending Late lists them. Approve or reject each one manually. They don't go through automatically.
Q: Can I undo a cancellation?
A: Yes. A refund banner appears with an undo button.

Driver & Logistics

Q: How do drivers know which customers to pick up first?
A: Pickups list in Taxi app is sorted by time. Each card shows resort (color), room number, activity.
Q: Can drivers mark pickups complete?
A: Yes. Button for each customer to mark them picked up. Finished pickups turn grey but stay visible.
Q: How does a driver know where a customer is?
A: Customers can share their live location (updates every 30 seconds). Map shows driver and customer position once driver is heading to them.
Q: Can drivers see instructors' locations?
A: Yes. Separate map shows current location of every instructor.
Q: Do drivers see return drop-offs separately?
A: Yes. Return drop-offs group by trip and sort by which boat arrives soonest.
