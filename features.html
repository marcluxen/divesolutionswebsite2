Platform-wide

These features work everywhere, in every app, for every role.

DiveTower apps diagram — how all the apps connect

Ten languages

Everything a customer sees is available in ten languages: English, Thai, French, German, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Chinese, Korean, and Japanese. This covers the booking form, confirmation emails, the customer app, and all waiver text. The system picks the right language automatically, based on what the customer chose. You can even set a different preparation video for each language, and each customer automatically sees the right one.

Who did what

The system keeps track of who did key actions, not just what happened. Every payment records who collected it. Every deleted booking records who deleted it, and why. Every salary payment records who marked it as paid. This makes it easy to check back later, if there is ever a question about something.

Keeping your data safe

Shop data is not stored on any one phone or computer. It is stored securely online. If a phone is lost or broken, no shop data is lost with it.

The system saves a full backup automatically, every 30 minutes. This backup is kept in a separate, secure location — not the same place the everyday data lives. This means there are always two safe copies.

Works everywhere, even offline

Every app installs to a phone's home screen, like a normal app. No app store is needed. The staff apps, and the public booking form, still work with a weak or lost connection. Actions are saved and sent once the connection comes back. Staff on a boat with bad signal are never blocked from doing their job.

Push notifications

Besides chat, DiveTower sends alerts to staff about things that need attention: a booking still missing an instructor, taxi, or boat for tomorrow; a late booking waiting for approval; a customer's insurance details, sent automatically before their dive day; and a review request, sent to customers after their trip.

Built for many shops, private to each one

DiveTower runs many dive shops on one platform at the same time. Each shop's data, staff, pricing, and branding stay completely separate. No shop can see another shop's data. New shops go through a guided setup process.

Master app

The owner's and manager's view. The whole shop, in one place.

The Day tab

The Day tab shows every booking for the day, each one a card or a row. It shows who is diving, their activity, their language, their instructor, how and when they get picked up, their resort, their meal, and which boat or trip they are on.

Some activities have many dives. You can give different dives to different instructors — one does the pool session, another does the boat dives.

If something important is missing, the field turns red: no instructor set, no driver set for a taxi pickup, no time set for a boat pickup, no resort, no meal, or no boat. Snorkeling is the exception — it is normal for snorkeling to have no instructor, so it does not turn red.

Day tab, showing a booking card with a red-flagged missing field

Staff can select many bookings at once — pay them all together, or move them all to a new date together.

Tapping a booking opens the full customer record: language, email, phone, nationality, age, date of birth, with a button to email the customer directly. Notes and who booked them. Gear sizes — height, weight, shoe size — and the app calculates BCD, wetsuit, and fin size from these numbers. Whether the customer brings their own gear. Their diving level, how many dives they have done, and their last dive. Their meal. Badges for liability waiver, medical form, and snorkel waiver — each shows signed or not signed. A clear warning badge shows if the customer has a medical issue. If a customer already has a doctor's fit-to-dive declaration, they can upload a photo of it instead of just ticking the medical box — even several pages. If a booking is cancelled, a refund banner appears, with a button to undo it. From this screen, staff can also move the booking to a new date, see all past payments, get a link to send the customer, or show a QR code for the customer's own app.

Customer details card — contact info, gear sizes, and waiver status badges

The Week tab

The Week tab shows the same booking information for a full week. It helps with planning ahead.

Week tab

Money Overview

All the shop's money information in one place. Four boxes are always shown, no matter which time period is selected: money paid today, money still owed by customers, money owed to agents and resorts, and refunds that still need a decision. If a customer pays an agent directly, that amount is removed from what the shop owes the agent — automatically.

Below the boxes, staff can pick a longer period: a week, a month, a year, or a custom range. A single day shows a simple list of payments. A longer period shows income and unpaid amounts broken down six ways: by activity, by payment method, by instructor, by nationality, by language, and by resort — each with a table and a bar chart. There is also a meal count, a payment method summary, totals for customers, revenue, paid, and open amounts, and a full history of past payouts.

Money Overview — charts and breakdown tables

Claims

Sometimes an instructor collects payment directly, out on the boat. When this happens, the instructor sends a claim saying what they collected — for one customer, or several at once. A master checks each claim: confirm it to record the payment properly, or dismiss it if it was a mistake. Until a master confirms it, the instructor can see that it is still waiting.

Claims list

Invoicing

Email a customer a proper invoice showing exactly what they still owe — always correct and up to date. Invoice several customers at once. If a group of bookings came from the same agent or resort, send one combined invoice listing every student, date, and activity in one document.

An invoice / agent statement

The Salary tab

For each instructor: what they are owed, and what is still pending. The owed list shows date, customer, activity, a small icon for dive progress, and the salary or commission amount, with a subtotal per instructor and one button to mark everything as paid. A second list shows work that is not finished yet — kept separate so it does not get mixed up with money that is ready to pay. When two instructors share one day, each is only paid for their own part of the work. If nothing shows on either list, a "why?" link explains the reason.

Salary tab, showing the dive-progress dots

The Search tab

Search customers using more than 20 fields. Dropdowns: nationality, language, dive level, meal, activity, booking source, who booked them, payment method, resort, instructor. Free text: name, email, phone, passport, room, notes. Yes/no filters: medical issue, certified, registered with agency, own gear, snorkel waiver signed, e-learning done.

Search tab and its results

The DiveAgency tab

Two simple lists: customers who still need agency registration, and customers who still need their certification finished. Each row shows name, activity, email, date of birth, date, and instructor, with a button to mark it done and a button to dismiss it.

DiveAgency to-do lists

The Gear tab

Shortage alerts at the top. Below that, the result of the shop's automatic gear allocation for the day. Below that, the full equipment list. If a customer's height, weight, or shoe size is missing, staff get an alert — so nobody is left without gear assigned.

Gear tab, showing the color-coded stock levels

The Private Boat tab

A form to book a whole boat for a group — separate from normal activity bookings, because people on a private boat may not have their activities decided yet. It asks for date, boat, an optional lead booker name, their email, their WhatsApp, number of people, the boat fee, and payment method.

Private Boat booking form

The Pickups tab

A live map showing every driver and every instructor's current position, updating every 30 seconds. There is also a simple list view.

Pickups tab — live map

Chat

Group channels for each role: Masters, Instructors, Taxi, Kitchen, Boat Crew. Direct one-to-one messages. Customer chats too — one for general shop questions, and a second that goes straight to the customer's current instructor. Chat supports photos, shows if a message has been read, shows an unread count in the app, and on phones that support it, a badge on the home screen icon.

Chat screen, showing the thread list

The QR tab

QR codes for the shop, for each instructor, and for each resort's own booking page.

QR tab

The top bar

Always visible, no matter which tab is open. It shows the current date and time, a button to turn on notifications, and a Refresh button — all working on phone and computer. On a computer screen, three more buttons appear. Print prints whatever tab is open. Export downloads the current tab's data as a spreadsheet file — works for the Day tab, Details, Gear, Salary, Money Overview, Shop Settings, and DiveAgency. A "suggest" button sends feedback straight to DiveTower. There is also a Roster button, which prints a clean roster for any single boat.

Shop Settings — 12 sections

Shop Settings menu grid

Team & Staff

Manages every role. Masters need a name, email, and password. Instructors also need: agency registration number, phone, WhatsApp, email, commission rate, nationality, languages, a photo, their own QR code, a toggle for logging in on days off, and a date range for when they are unavailable. Drivers are the same, but instead of languages they have a maximum passenger number. Boat crew and kitchen staff just need name, phone, WhatsApp, email, and password. Boat types are set up here too: name and passenger capacity. Each role gets its own QR code, so a new staff member can install their app right away.

Team & Staff settings screen

Resorts & Agents

Stores each resort's pickup time, a color (used to group them in the app), email, an automatic booking link and QR code, and their commission rate. Agents get a name, email, and commission rate. A separate table holds the shop's general commission rates, which can be changed for a specific resort or a specific activity. Staff can also enter a booking from an agent or resort using a fast form: pick the agent, pick the date, and list the people — with a shortcut to add several people at once if they are all doing the same activity and do not need to be named yet.

Resorts & Agents settings screen

Activities & Pricing

Sets up each activity: price, number of days, salary, certification cost, which agency it belongs to, whether it needs another certification first, number of dives, and salary per dive.

Activities & Pricing settings screen

Special Fee Day

For marine park or national park fees. Set one or more date ranges, each with its own pattern — ranges cannot overlap. A booking always keeps the fee that was active on its own date, even if the rules change later. Separate fees for diving and snorkeling, each split into four amounts: local adult, local child, foreign adult, foreign child. The fee can also be turned on or off manually for any single day.

Special Fee Day settings screen

Payments

Every payment method the shop accepts: cash, QR codes, and five online providers (Stripe, PayPal, PayMongo, Opn, HitPay). Each online provider needs a connection link, set up once. This section also has the shop's currency (six choices), and manual exchange rates for US dollars and euros.

Payments settings screen

Day Templates

Plan each day of a multi-day course. The plan becomes a checklist that instructors check off as the course happens.

Day Templates settings screen

Gear & Equipment

Tracks every item and size the shop owns: total count, broken/unusable count, and available count — calculated automatically, with a color warning when low. It also shows the rental price.

Gear & Equipment settings screen

Booking Sources

Holds the shop's meal list and booking source list, plus the settings for reading booking emails automatically from other platforms, and the email address the shop uses for that.

Booking Sources settings screen

Waivers

Covers the cancellation policy: how many days' notice is needed, and the time cutoff. Staff can see exactly what text the customer will read. Shops can upload their own waiver documents instead of using the built-in ones. All waiver text is available in every supported language.

Waivers settings screen, including the cancellation policy preview

Course Videos

Add a course video for each activity, in each language. If one combination has no video, the system falls back to a sensible choice. Skill demonstration videos — mask clearing, regulator recovery, and the rest — are managed here too, and instructors can re-send any of them to a student.

Course Videos settings screen

Locations

Manages the shop's map. Each business added gets a name, a category, a pin on the map, a description, and an optional discount code. Shops create their own categories, each with an emoji and a name.

Locations settings screen and map

Shop Info & Account

Holds: certifying agency, shop name, WhatsApp number, email sender name, main email, a separate email for insurance notices, timezone, and links to Google reviews and Google Maps.

Shop Info & Account settings screen

Trash

Holds deleted bookings. When staff delete a booking, they can leave a short reason why. Deleted bookings can be restored, or deleted forever. If the customer's money was kept and not refunded, staff can mark this — so the income still counts in the shop's reports.

Trash list

Pending Late

A list of bookings made after the shop's daily cutoff time. A master must approve or reject each one — they do not confirm automatically.

Pending Late list

Instructor app

My Trips

Groups the instructor's day by boat and time. It shows each student's progress, using the shop's own names for each dive — not generic names. If the instructor is not the lead on a trip, it shows the real boatmaster's name instead. A one-tap button sends an estimated return time; once sent, it cannot be changed or sent again.

Tapping a student's card opens their details: meal and dietary note, certification level, number of dives, nationality, age, height, weight, shoe size, and notes. If the booking is cancelled, a refund banner appears with an undo button; if not, there is a button to start a refund. The dives section shows which boat the student is on. Below that is the checklist: confined water, each numbered dive, and the night dive — each one can have its own instructor, different from the day's main instructor. Any custom dive types also appear here. A waivers section shows if liability, safe-diving, medical, and youth waivers are signed. For certification courses, there is a section for e-learning, agency registration, the agency code, and final certification — with digital PADI and SSI forms handled right in the app. Students on the SSI Open Water course also get their full completion record here: every session and skill, plus both signatures. Payment is shown for reference only — actually collecting money happens in a separate Upgrade/QR tab, not here.

Boat crew app

The boat crew app is small — by design. Besides chat, it has two things: the same trip roster that instructors and masters see, and a pickups view with a live driver map. There is nothing else. A small, focused app.

Boat crew app

Customer app

After booking, every customer gets their own private app. No account, no password, no app store — just a personal link. It covers their whole trip.

Pickup tab

Shows their next pickup time, once one is set. A button lets them share their live location, so the driver can find them. Once a driver is on the way, a map appears showing the driver's position and their own, so they can watch the driver approach. It also shows their past pickups.

Customer app Pickup tab — map view

My Diving tab

The tab with the most information. A preparation video, matched to their language — with a sensible fallback if there is no exact match. Alongside it, skill demonstration videos — mask clearing, regulator recovery, and the other skills — so a student can watch a skill again the evening before, as many times as they like. An instructor can also re-send any video to a student who needs another look. Together with their digital paperwork in the same app, students arrive prepared. A QR code (and link) confirming agency certification, when relevant. A day-by-day schedule: which boat and which instructor each day. A progress section: e-learning status if they are registered, a checkmark for each finished dive, and an overall percentage. Their gear: either "own gear," or their exact BCD, wetsuit, fin, and weightbelt sizes. SSI Open Water students also see their own copy of the completion record here — they cannot edit it, except to add their own signature (and clear it, if added by mistake).

Customer app My Diving tab — progress bar and schedule

Locations tab

A map with the shop's own location always pinned. Every other business the shop has added is shown too, each with its own category icon. Below the map, the same places are listed in full, with description and discount code shown directly — not hidden behind the pin.

Public booking form

A customer booking online picks a date, then adds one or more people to the booking. Each person needs a name, an email, and an activity. Prices show live as they pick. They can also add rental gear. If an activity needs an existing certification, the form checks this automatically — it will not let them continue until they confirm it.

First step of the booking form — adding people and picking activities

A review screen then shows each person's price, any rentals, the total, the date, and the payment method — before they pay. Checkout supports five payment providers, so a shop does not depend on just one. If someone tries to book too late in the evening for a same-day or next-day slot, the system explains why, and offers the next date that is actually free.

Booking form review screen

Staff booking a customer in person get two extra things: a lookup that finds returning customers by name and fills in their details, and the ability to add a discount.
