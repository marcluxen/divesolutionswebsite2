Overview of all apps: Master, Instructors, Drivers, Kitchen, Boat Crew, Customers

Master (Owner/Manager)

Complete control of your shop, one system. See everything happening, make decisions fast.

Daily Operations

Day view with all bookings, auto-flagged gaps (missing instructor, driver, boat, resort, meal). Create daily trips, bulk edit, full audit trail.

Money & Salaries

Money Overview with paid/owed/commissions/refunds. Instructor salaries calculated automatic per dive. 6-way breakdowns by activity, method, instructor, nationality, language, resort.

Customers & Search

Search 20+ fields (name, email, phone, nationality, level, certification, medical flag). Find anyone fast. View full customer record with certifications, gear sizes, waivers.

Customer Reviews & Certifications

Automatic review requests after each dive. See reviews by instructor and activity. Track pending registrations and certifications with agencies. One-click to Google.

Gear Management

Automatic gear allocation by customer size. Shortage alerts — never leave someone standing. Full inventory synced from ScubaKit every 15 minutes.

Shop Settings

Team & staff setup, resorts, agents, activities, pricing, waivers, videos, locations, marine park fees, payment methods, booking sources, cancellation policy.

Instructors

Everything you need in the water and on the boat. Students, gear, checklist, money all in one app.

My Students

Full details for each student: meal, dietary notes, gear sizes, certifications, number of dives. See exactly what each student still needs today (waivers, e-learning, registration, payment).

Dive Checklist

Day-by-day checklist matched to your activity: confined, numbered dives, night dive. Check each off as you go. Different instructor per dive if needed. SSI completion records built in.

Gear & Allocations

Your students' sizes or own gear. All boats for the day grouped. Shop-wide shortages flagged. Full inventory synced live.

Collect Money & Upgrades

See student balances. Collect payment direct or show QR code. Upgrade students on the spot with new price. One-tap to record what was paid and how.

Salary Breakdown

What you're owed: salary and commission per student, broken down per dive. Separate section for work not yet marked done.

Day Roster & Chat

See every trip, all instructors, all pax — not just yours. Group chat by role. Direct messages. Chat with your students.

Taxi Drivers

Know exactly who to pick up, when, where. Live location tracking. Call or WhatsApp direct.

Pickup List

Sorted by time. Resort, room number, activity for each. One-tap call or WhatsApp. Mark picked up. Finished pickups turn grey.

Return Drop-offs

Grouped by trip, sorted by arrival time. One-tap to mark dropped off.

Live Maps

Customer locations (if shared) update every 30 seconds. Instructor locations too. Watch where everyone is.

Team Chat

Group channels by role. Direct messages to masters, instructors, crew.

Kitchen Staff

See exactly how many meals you need, today and this month. Dietary notes for every customer.

Live Meal Counts

Today's counts and monthly counts. Dietary notes per customer. Auto-refreshes every 2 minutes.

Boat Crew

Full trip roster. See every boat, every instructor, every pax, tank counts. Pickup tracking.

Trip Roster

Every boat for the day. Pax count, tank count. All instructors, all students. Who's going out with who.

Pickups & Maps

Live driver map updates every 30 seconds. Know when crew's heading back.

Team Chat

Group channels, direct messages.

Customers

Everything they need before, during, and after their dive. Transparent, simple, and reassuring.

Pickup Tracking

Know when pickup is set. Share your location so driver finds you. Watch the driver approach on live map once they're on the way.

Prep & Learning

Watch prep video auto-matched to your language and agency. Quick theory summary with practice questions. Learn at your own pace before you arrive.

Course Progress

Day-by-day schedule: which boat, which instructor. Checkmark each dive as it's done. E-learning status. SSI completion record you can sign yourself.

Waivers & Forms

Sign liability, medical, and youth waivers on your own phone. Upload medical declaration if you have one. All in your language.

Gear & Details

See your gear assigned: BCD, wetsuit, fins, weightbelt calculated from your size. Or confirm you're bringing your own. Edit your details any time.

Payment & Chat

See what you owe at any time. Message the shop or your instructor directly. Ask questions, get answers fast.

Local Info

Map of nearby spots: resorts, dive sites, restaurants, shops. Discount codes built in.
