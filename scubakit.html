ScubaKit — equipment tracking

Never leave a customer standing because the gear isn't there.

ScubaKit keeps track of every piece of equipment your shop owns — what you have, what size, what works, what's in service, and what's due for inspection. Connected to your bookings, so tomorrow's gear problems show up today.

Included in DiveTower — no extra cost

Your whole equipment room, in your pocket

Every item, counted and sized

BCDs, wetsuits, fins, regulators, tanks, weights — every item and size the shop owns, with a total count, a broken/unusable count, and an available count calculated automatically. A color warning shows when a size runs low, before it becomes a problem on the pier.

Equipment list with color-coded stock levels

Gear matched to tomorrow's customers

Because ScubaKit is connected to DiveTower's bookings, it knows who is diving tomorrow and what sizes they need. Gear is allocated automatically from each customer's height, weight, and shoe size — and if there's a shortage, or a customer's sizes are missing, you get an alert with time to fix it. Nobody stands on the boat without a wetsuit that fits.

Automatic gear allocation for the day, with a shortage alert

Servicing and inspections, never forgotten

Every regulator service, every tank inspection, on a schedule. Cylinders get a full inspection form modeled on the international standard (ISO 18119), with hydro and visual inspection dates tracked separately. When a hydro date expires, the tank is blocked from use until it's inspected — the system enforces it, so no out-of-date tank slips onto a boat.

Cylinder inspection form and service history

A clear status for every item

Each item shows its status at a glance: in use, in service, or retired. Send a regulator away for service and it disappears from the available count until it's back. Scrap an old BCD and it leaves the books properly, with the history kept.

Equipment status pills — in use, in service, retired

Faults reported from the boat, with photos

A leaking inflator, a torn strap — staff report a fault the moment they see it, photo attached, from any phone. The item is flagged, the count updates, and the fix doesn't depend on someone remembering to mention it back at the shop.

Fault report with photo

Reminders that come to you

Push notifications for each service type, so upcoming inspections and services announce themselves. You don't check the system — the system tells you.

Service reminder notification

Rental income, tracked

Every item carries its rental price, and rentals added to a booking flow straight into the customer's bill and the shop's money overview. Rental gear earns properly, instead of leaving the shop for free.

Part of the platform, not an add-on.

ScubaKit comes included with DiveTower at $99/month — one system where your bookings, your customers, and your equipment room all know about each other.
